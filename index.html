First-time priority

Review and agree to the Terms of Service first. Then complete System Settings before building categories, menu items and modifiers.

First task

Merchant Platform Agreement

Before using the platform to operate your business, review the Merchant Platform Agreement and agree to the Terms of Service.

How to review the agreement

  1. From Admin Home, locate the Merchant Platform Agreement card.
  2. Select View to open the reference copy of the current agreement.
  3. Review the Terms of Service and confirm that you understand the obligations that apply to your business.
  4. Agree to the Terms of Service before continuing with System Settings or other operational setup.
Important: The Merchant Platform Agreement card provides a reference copy of the current agreement. Keep a record of the version you reviewed according to your business practices.

Setup step 2

System Settings

After agreeing to the Terms of Service, enter your restaurant identity, branded banner image, address, taxes, payment preferences, operating hours, delivery choices, contact details, and authorized administrators.

Restaurant and brand

  1. Upload a Hero Image to represent your business on the ordering page. The portal resizes it automatically.
  2. Enter the customer-facing restaurant Name.
  3. Enter your Address, City, State, and ZIP.
  4. Do not edit the Restaurant ID; it uniquely identifies your business in the system.

Taxes and fees

Order acceptance and operating hours

Cash payments

Decide whether you accept cash payments. Enable cash only when your team can collect and reconcile cash orders reliably.

Hours of operation

Enter your opening and closing hours. Orders are unavailable after closing, helping prevent orders from arriving when you are not operating.

Own delivery

Select whether you handle your own deliveries. Turn this on only when your business will fulfill delivery orders directly.

Delivery service fee

If you handle your own deliveries, create and manage the related delivery Service Fee in the Service Fees section.

Operating tip: Before opening for the day, confirm cash acceptance, operating hours, and delivery status. If you deliver yourself, verify the delivery Service Fee before accepting delivery orders.

Contact and administrators

  1. Enter the primary contact’s Name, Email, and Phone.
  2. Add only trusted team members under Authorized Administrators.
  3. Select Remove when someone should no longer access the portal.
Operator tip: Confirm taxes and the “Patron pays” or “Operator pays” choice before displaying QR codes. These settings affect checkout totals and your margin.

Step 3

Menu Items

Build the menu in order: create a category such as Breakfast, Lunch or Beverages, then add each item and assign it to that category. Set and edit photos and pricing here.

Menu Item Categories

Create Menu Item Categories before adding individual items, or create them through the Menu Items CSV workflow. Every menu item should be assigned to the correct category.

Build clear categories

  • Examples: Breakfast, Lunch and Beverages.
  • Keep names short, clear and consistent.
  • Use the dedicated category editor or the menu-item CSV workflow.

Maintain menu structure

  • Open Menu Item Categories from Admin Home.
  • Add or edit categories as the menu changes.
  • Check item assignments before deleting a category.

Create and edit menu items

  1. Open Menu Item Categories and create a category such as Breakfast, Lunch or Beverages.
  2. Open Menu Items, enter an item name, choose the category and enter its price.
  3. Add a description and image if appropriate, then save the item.
  4. Use Select to edit the item’s category, description, photo, price, availability and modifiers. Save with Update Item.
  5. Use Delete only for items you no longer offer.

Import menu items with CSV

  1. Prepare a CSV with name, category and price headers.
  2. Categories can also be created during the menu-item CSV import; verify names and prices before uploading.
  3. Upload the CSV and check the imported items, categories, images and prices.

Sample CSV

name,category,price
Fried Eggs,Breakfast,8.99
Aqua Fresca,Drinks,4.50
Carne en Mole,Mexican,24.99
CSV validation: Every item must pass validation or the entire file is rejected. Existing items are skipped. Test a small CSV first.

Step 4

Modifiers: types and choices

Modifier Types → Modifiers → Select on items

1. Modifier Types / Tipos / 类型

  1. Open Menu Item Modifier Types. Create a group such as Ingredients, Flavors or Preparation using Add Modifier Type.

2. Modifiers / Modificadores / 加选项

  1. Open Menu Item Modifiers. Enter description, unique slug, type and price. Use $0.00 for no surcharge; a chosen modifier price adds to the base item price.
  2. Bulk upload CSV headers: slug, description, type, price.

3. Select / Seleccionar / 选择

Modifier Types → Modifiers → Select on items — Modifiers link automatically to menu items but must be selected in each item editor and saved with Update Item before patrons see them. A $14.50 item plus a $4.25 selection totals $18.75 before tax and fees.

Step 5

Orders: view and refunds

Use Orders for full, line-item or verified custom refunds.

Review orders / Revisar pedidos / 查看订单

  1. Review quantities, modifiers, customer notes, payment state and delivery address.
  2. Print if needed. Complete only after fulfillment; cancel when the order cannot be fulfilled.

Refunds / Reembolsos / 退款

  1. In Orders, open the original order and select Process refund.
  2. Choose Full refund for the total, Refund Line Item(s) for specific items, or a partial/custom option if the deployed interface permits a set amount.
  3. Verify the original charge and amount; select Process Refund, then confirm the result in the order and report.
The screenshot labels the third option Goodwill Refund, not Set Amount. Only use it for a set figure if your deployed interface supports entering that amount.

Step 6

Sales Reports

Expand Sales Reports tickets for line items and refunds.

Review ticket details / Detalle / 票据明细

  1. Select Sales Reports → View from Admin Home.
  2. Find a ticket by order ID, date, place or customer; click the plus sign to expand its itemized rows.
  3. Check quantities, prices, taxes, fees and any refund entries; use Download CSV for reconciliation.

Step 7

Service Fees

Set a fixed delivery charge; verify the mileage control in the deployed app.

Delivery fee / Tarifa de entrega / 配送费

  1. Open Service Fees → Edit. Enter Name, unique Slug, customer-facing Description and fixed Price.
  2. Set the maximum delivery distance in miles if a delivery range control exists in your deployed configuration. Test addresses inside and outside the range.
  3. Use Apply to all orders only if that is the intended policy; otherwise leave the charge delivery-specific. Save and test checkout.
The supplied Service Fees screenshot does not show a maximum-mileage field. Verify the live distance control before promising a delivery range. This fixed fee is different from the percentage service fee in System Settings.

Setup step 4

Places and QR Codes

Places still provide customer ordering locations and QR codes. Set up each applicable truck, counter, table or delivery location and test its QR link before service.

Create a Place

  1. Enter a clear location name, such as “Truck – Downtown,” “Friday Market,” or “Table 12.”
  2. Leave Add to all menu items selected when the location should offer your full menu.
  3. Select Add, then find the location in Current Places.

Download and test the QR code

  1. Select Download QR for the correct Place.
  2. Print it on a durable sign, table tent, counter card, window decal, or event menu board.
  3. Scan it with a phone before service and confirm it opens the right ordering page.

Menu availability and local taxes

  • Use Remove from menu items, then Save, if a Place should not offer menu items.
  • Enter Local Tax Location and Local Tax Rate when a Place has its own tax treatment, then select Save.
  • Use Remove for an inactive location and remove its physical QR sign from service.
Example: Create “Oakland Truck” and “San Francisco Festival” as separate Places. Display only the matching QR code at each active location.
