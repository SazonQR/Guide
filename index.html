Orientation

Start at the Admin Home

The Admin Home is your operations dashboard. Its six cards provide direct access to menu categories, menu items, live orders, system settings, order records, and ordering locations.

Recommended setup sequence

  1. Complete System Settings.
  2. Create Menu Item Categories.
  3. Add or import Menu Items.
  4. Create Places and download QR codes.
  5. Monitor Orders during service.
  6. Review Order History after service.

Use the six sections

  • Edit opens business or menu setup.
  • View / Edit opens the live order board.
  • View opens completed and canceled records.
  • Manage opens location and QR-code controls.

Section 1

System Settings

Enter your restaurant identity, branded banner image, address, taxes, fee selection, contact details, and authorized administrators.

Restaurant and brand

  1. Upload a Hero Image to represent your business on the ordering page. The portal resizes it automatically.
  2. Enter the customer-facing restaurant Name.
  3. Enter your Address, City, State, and ZIP.
  4. Do not edit the Restaurant ID; it uniquely identifies your business in the system.

Taxes and fees

FieldWhat to doWhy it matters
State TaxEnter your applicable state sales-tax percentage.It contributes to the order tax calculation.
Local TaxEnter your applicable local tax percentage.It adds the relevant local component to orders.
Fee OptionChoose Patron pays or Operator pays.This determines who absorbs the platform fee.
Service feeEnter a percentage only if you intend to charge a service fee.It adds that percentage to applicable orders.

Contact and administrators

  1. Enter the primary contact’s Name, Email, and Phone.
  2. Add only trusted team members under Authorized Administrators.
  3. Select Remove when someone should no longer access the portal.
Operator tip: Confirm taxes and the “Patron pays” or “Operator pays” choice before displaying QR codes. These settings affect checkout totals and your margin.

Section 2

Menu Items

Create, update, and remove individual products, or import multiple products through a CSV file.

Add or manage one item

  1. Enter the product Name.
  2. Select a Category, or create the category through the menu-item workflow if needed.
  3. Enter the Price ($).
  4. Upload a clear food photo under Picture when available.
  5. Select Add Item. Use Select for an existing product and Delete only for products you no longer offer.

Import menu items by CSV

  1. Create a CSV with headers name, category, and price.
  2. Use the category column to assign products. Categories can also be created through the Menu Items CSV workflow.
  3. Check each row for a valid name, category, and price.
  4. Select the file, then choose Upload Items.

Sample CSV

name,category,price
Fried Eggs,Breakfast,8.99
Aqua Fresca,Drinks,4.50
Carne en Mole,Mexican,24.99
CSV validation: Every item must pass validation or the entire file is rejected. Existing items are skipped. Test a small CSV first.

Section 3

Places and QR Codes

A Place is a named ordering location, such as your truck, a counter, a table, or an event. Each Place has its own customer-facing QR code.

Create a Place

  1. Enter a clear location name, such as “Truck – Downtown,” “Friday Market,” or “Table 12.”
  2. Leave Add to all menu items selected when the location should offer your full menu.
  3. Select Add, then find the location in Current Places.

Download and test the QR code

  1. Select Download QR for the correct Place.
  2. Print it on a durable sign, table tent, counter card, window decal, or event menu board.
  3. Scan it with a phone before service and confirm it opens the right ordering page.

Menu availability and local taxes

  • Use Remove from menu items, then Save, if a Place should not offer menu items.
  • Enter Local Tax Location and Local Tax Rate when a Place has its own tax treatment, then select Save.
  • Use Remove for an inactive location and remove its physical QR sign from service.
Example: Create “Oakland Truck” and “San Francisco Festival” as separate Places. Display only the matching QR code at each active location.

Section 4

Menu Item Categories

Use categories to organize the customer menu. Manage categories here, or create and upload them through the Menu Items CSV workflow.

Build clear categories

  • Use customer-friendly labels such as Tacos, Plates, Breakfast, Drinks, and Desserts.
  • Keep names short, distinct, and easy to scan.
  • Create or upload categories in this section or while importing menu items by CSV.

Maintain menu structure

  • Open Menu Item Categories from Admin Home.
  • Add, rename, or remove categories as your menu changes.
  • Before deleting a category, reassign items that use it.
Recommended practice: Avoid near-duplicate categories such as “Drink,” “Drinks,” and “Beverages.” Consistent names make menus clearer and CSV imports more reliable.

Section 5

Orders

Orders is your live service board. Monitor pending orders, payment state, location, customer notes, and fulfillment progress.

Read the order board

On-screen itemHow to use it
History filtersUse Canceled or Completed to review past outcomes.
Pending filtersUse All or a Place filter to focus the active production queue.
Order agePrioritize older orders during high-volume service.
Payment stateConfirm whether the order is Paid or Awaiting Payment before fulfillment.
NotesRead customer notes before preparing food.

Process an order

  1. Keep Orders open for the team during service.
  2. Verify items, quantity, Place, payment state, and notes.
  3. Prepare the order once payment requirements are satisfied.
  4. Choose Print when a kitchen or pickup ticket is useful.
  5. Choose Complete when the order is fulfilled and ready or handed to the customer.
  6. Choose Cancel only when the order cannot be fulfilled.
Service control: Do not mark an “Awaiting Payment” order complete unless payment has been separately confirmed under your business procedure.

Section 6

Order History

Order History stores completed and canceled transactions. Use it for end-of-day review, reconciliation, customer questions, and sales analysis.

Review and export

Records can include date and time, customer, items, Place, subtotal, tax, tip, order total, payment method, service fee, amount charged, and status. Select Download CSV to export the sales record for a service period and use it in your accounting workflow.

Closing practice: Save exports using a clear date range, such as MyFoodTruck-Sales-2026-09-16.csv, to make reconciliation easier.

Practical checklist

Daily operating routine

Before opening

  • Confirm system settings, tax rates, and fee selection.
  • Ensure the menu reflects current availability.
  • Display and test the correct Place QR code.
  • Open the live Orders board.

During service

  • Prioritize older paid orders.
  • Check item details, Place, and notes before preparing food.
  • Print tickets when useful.
  • Mark orders complete promptly after fulfillment.

After closing

  • Review completed and canceled orders.
  • Download the Order History CSV.
  • Reconcile payments and orders.
  • Update sold-out items and retire inactive QR codes.

Quick fixes

  • Missing item: confirm its category and Place availability.
  • Wrong QR: download and replace it with the correct Place QR code.
  • CSV issue: validate every name, category, and price field.
  • Routing issue: check the Place on the order card and the QR sign on-site.
